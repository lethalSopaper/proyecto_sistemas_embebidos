XIAO ESP32-S3 PINOUT CONNECTIONS

1. POWER (Shared)
- 3.3V Pin ---> OLED VCC  +  GPS VCC
- GND Pin  ---> OLED GND  +  GPS GND

2. OLED DISPLAY
- Pin D8   ---> OLED SDA
- Pin D7   ---> OLED SCL

3. NEO-6M GPS
- Pin D1   ---> GPS TX
- Pin D2   ---> GPS RX

4. MODE BUTTON
- Pin D0   ---> Push button ---> GND
- Internal pull-up enabled in firmware
- Button press toggles between GPS monitoring and WiFi/MQTT upload mode

5. OTHER (Do not use)
- Pin D4   ---> Occupied by green wire
- Pin D5   ---> Occupied by green wire
